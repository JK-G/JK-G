# Jakub Kowalik

GenAI Product Owner at Wenet, based in Poland. I took Wenet's AI assistant from an early proof of concept to production, and I build my own tools for coding agents along the way.

## What I do at Wenet

I own Wenet Asystent AI, a production LLM assistant that answers small businesses' customer inquiries. I took it over as an early proof of concept and led it through pilot, launch in April 2026 and growth. My side is the product: discovery, conversation flows, UI and user flows, specs, pricing and packaging, release and evals. Two developers build it with me.

Before a bigger feature goes to the developers, I build a working prototype on my own branch with Claude Code. They get something they can run, plus a spec with the business assumptions and acceptance criteria. Production architecture stays with them.

I also designed and implemented the evaluation harness for the assistant with Claude Code: synthetic customer profiles (broken and incomplete ones too), dual-judge scoring and regression tests across many scenarios.
